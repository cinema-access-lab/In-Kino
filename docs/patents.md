# Patents and prior art

A record of our own patent checks. It is not a legal opinion or a freedom-to-operate
analysis.

## The fingerprinting method

In-Kino identifies the film position with spectral landmark fingerprinting: pairs of
spectrogram peaks hashed with their time difference. The method was published by Avery
Wang in 2003 ("An Industrial-Strength Audio Search Algorithm", ISMIR 2003). Its original
patent, US 6,990,453 (filed 2001), is listed as expired. The European and German
counterparts (EP 1 307 833, DE 601 20 417) were filed in 2001, so their maximum 20-year
term ended by 2021.

## Before a public release

Before the app is released to audiences, we plan a professional freedom-to-operate search
by a patent attorney. Prototyping and testing fall under the exemptions for private and
experimental use in § 11 of the German Patent Act. Publishing the code as open source also
makes it prior art against later patents on the same methods.
